## Opening and closing

While the panel is closed, its screens are a narrow column on the right,
each F-key with a short name. Press an F-key or click one to open the
panel on that screen, beside the agent. Click the open one again to close
the panel. While the panel is kept open, the column gives way to a row of
the screens above it.

`F1`–`F5` open and focus a screen; `ctrl+q e 1`…`e 5` do the same, and
`m` in the panel cycles the screens. `esc`, `ctrl+q a a` or a click gives
the keyboard back to the agent. Press `?` in the panel for all of its
commands.

| After `ctrl+q` | Action |
|---|---|
| `e 1`…`e 5` | Open code changes, GitHub, tasks, skills or plugins |
| `z e` | Show / hide the panel |
| `z k` | Small panel, which folds again when you go back to the agent / keep it open |
| `z z` | Make the panel full width |
| `<` / `>` | Widen / narrow the panel |
| `z l` | Choose a layout |

<details>
<summary>F-keys in terminals and on Mac laptops</summary>

In a terminal pane at its prompt, F1–F5 still switch screens. A
full-screen program there (htop, mc, vim) gets the function keys for
itself: use `ctrl+q e 1`…`e 5` then. F6 and up always go to the program.

A Mac laptop may need `fn` for the F-keys. Set `function_keys = false`
to label the screens with their `ctrl+q e` shortcuts instead.

</details>

## Small, open or full width

- **Small:** click `»` at the top of the panel, or press `ctrl+q z k`.
  The panel opens when you pick a screen and folds again when the
  keyboard goes back to the agent. Good for a quick look.
- **Kept open:** click `«`. The panel stays beside the agent, as wide as
  `panel_width` (50% by default). Drag the divider to resize it.
- **Full width:** `ctrl+q z z`, for reading a large change.

A panel kept open makes room for the tab's panes: while they would not
all fit beside it, it becomes small by itself, and opens again once they
fit. Click `«` to keep it open anyway.

## Layouts

`ctrl+q z l` lets you try the layouts: the arrows preview, `enter` saves
and `esc` restores. Set `layout` to start in one:

| `layout` | At start |
|---|---|
| `normal` | The agent, and the panel beside it |
| `wide` | The agent with three quarters of the window |
| `small` | The panel closed, folding again after a look |
| `agent` | The agent alone, the panel closed |
| `panel` | The panel full width |

`ctrl+q z =` puts the agent left and the panel right at `panel_width`,
`ctrl+q z +` gives the agent three quarters, and `ctrl+q z a` shows the
agent alone.

## Asking the agent from the panel

Requests from the panel go to the tab's agent as messages, delivered when
it is idle. Most open as drafts for you to read, edit and send.

Read drafts before sending. Review comments and CI logs contain text from
others, which may include instructions meant for the agent, and the agent
acts within its permissions.
